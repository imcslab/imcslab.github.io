Hansung University · Division of Computer Engineering

Intelligent Mobility and Cybersecurity Lab.

The IMCS Lab conducts pioneering research in intelligent mobility, physical AI and cybersecurity. Under the guidance of Prof. Seunghyun Park, we work on securing connected and autonomous vehicles — from in-vehicle intrusion detection and risk assessment methodology to EV charging authentication — and on physical AI that perceives, decides and knows when not to act, creating solutions that impact both academia and industry.

Research themes Publications

Research topics

Our work spans automotive cybersecurity technology and regulation, physical AI and intelligent mobility, and cybersecurity policy and technology.

Track 01

Automotive Security

  • Connected vehicle security
  • Threat Analysis and Risk Assessment (TARA)
  • In-vehicle security (CAN IDS, gateway, Ethernet firewalls)
  • Plug & Charge security (ISO 15118)
  • Automotive software update over-the-air (OTA)
  • Blockchain-based automotive security
  • Regulation and compliance (UN R155/R156, ISO/SAE 21434, SAE J3061, ISO 24089)

Track 02

Physical AI & Mobility

  • Capability-aware safe meta-decision for physical AI robotics (Try-Ask-Refuse)
  • Cross-domain perception
  • LLM/VLM-based multimodal decision systems
  • Multi-sensor fusion and adaptive point cloud processing
  • Trajectory planning for collision avoidance

Track 03

Cybersecurity Policy & Technology

  • Secure software development life cycle
  • Crisis management and incident response
  • Public cloud security
  • Web and mobile security inspection
  • ISO/IEC 27001
